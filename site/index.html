Interpretation rules

  1. Main quality charts use only completed temperature-zero, 92-case rounds. Temperature-one observations and the older 69-case results sit in separate appendices below, on their own axes, and never share a median.
  2. Every result's score and elapsed time is shown. Medians and observed ranges exist only for repeated cohorts; single rounds have no variance estimate. A missing measurement is shown as missing, never as zero.
  3. A quality/time point is one completed tool-eval round — the wall time of one 92-scenario phase — excluding model boot, preparation, hardware queue waits and admission probes. Effort, output cap, concurrency and TP size stay attached to every point.
  4. RigMark and SparkBench are distinct metrics. Decode rate, aggregate throughput, cold and warm TTFT, and the two staggered-arrival directions each keep their own label and unit.
  5. Quality and speed used different sources in places. RiNGSiDE tool-eval used RedHatAI weights at both TP sizes; the historical TP2 speed profile used NVIDIA weights. A lab-plan hash is not a Git commit. No identical-configuration quality/speed claim is made.
  6. Truncation and campaign status are preserved. Reported output-cap truncations stay flagged; owner-stopped (Tony2wild) and cancelled/failed (Knapcio) parent campaigns are not relabelled successful. An absent truncation report does not prove the cap was never reached.
  7. Knapcio is the measured dddb0347 switchless port. Its native template ignores thinking-off, so equivalent effective thinking behaviour is unproved.
  8. This compares complete historical recipes, not an isolated quantisation, kernel, transport or universal model-quality effect.
  9. Warm replay is a cached-prefix measurement, not full-token prefill. RigMark's warm replay re-sends the same prompt, so the server may reuse its prefix cache. RiNGSiDE's plans document prefix-cache retention and a replay boundary. No packaged receipt documents speculative prefill; it is left unknown. A separate speculative-prefill recall example (36/36 facts recalled with full prefill versus 11/36 with speculative prefill, Qwen3.8 Flash Next Q5 on an M5 Ultra) is not a RiNGSiDE measurement and its numbers do not transfer here.
